About

We're a husband and wife, and this is our shop.

The phrases come from family group chats, kitchen tables, and the way we actually talk. They're not slogans we found later. They were already there.

We started putting them on things that stay in the house: a mug, a shirt, a tote, a onesie, a print by the door. Things you reach for without thinking.

Each piece is printed after it's ordered. No closet full of leftover stock. We design, you order, then it gets made and ships.

If something here doesn't feel ready, that's because we're still adding. Come back.
