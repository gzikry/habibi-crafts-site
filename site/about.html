Our story

It started with the words.

Yalla when it is time to go. Sahtein before a meal. Khalas when everyone knows the conversation should have ended five minutes ago.

Small pieces of home, made useful.

The phrases come from family group chats, kitchen tables, and the way we actually talk. They are not slogans we found later. They were already there.

Habibi Crafts Co puts those words on things that earn their place in everyday life: the mug beside the coffee machine, a baby gift that makes the whole family smile, a print by the front door.

Each piece will be made after it is ordered. We are building the collection slowly, checking the artwork and product fit before opening checkout.
