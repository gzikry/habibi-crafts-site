About

A small shop in California. We print mugs, tees, totes, onesies, and prints, one at a time.

It started with things we say out loud. Yalla at the door. Sahtein at the table. Khalas, usually with a hand up, when the conversation is finished.

Words like that don't translate cleanly. Ya Aini is what you call someone you're fond of, often while handing them a cup of tea. Ya Dunia is what you say when the day is being a lot. You either know them or you're about to.

So we put them on things people actually use. A mug that gets refilled three times before noon. A tote that goes to the market every Saturday. A onesie that will be outgrown before it's outworn.

The Arabic is the part we started with, not the only part. There are designs in the shop with no Arabic on them at all, and more coming from other places and other kitchens. If something looks good and means something, it belongs here.

How it works

01

You pick the phrase

Every piece is one design. No variants to scroll past, no colour combinations to sort through.

02

It gets printed

Nothing sits in a warehouse. Your mug or shirt is made after you order it, then packed and shipped to your door.

03

It arrives

Mugs, totes, and prints ship in a few days. Apparel takes a little longer because it's printed and pressed to order.
