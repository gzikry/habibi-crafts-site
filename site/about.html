Why this exists

Some phrases deserve a place on the shelf.

The words we grew up with, put on things we actually use.

This shop started with the words that show up in everyday life: yalla when it is time to go, sahtein before a meal, and khalas when the conversation is over. We are putting those small pieces of home on useful things, one design at a time.

The phrases come from family group chats, kitchen tables, and the way we actually talk. They are not slogans. They are the small words that follow us from one room to the next.

Each piece is made after you order. We do not keep a room full of finished stock. If you are looking for a mug for a friend, a little something for a new baby, or a sign for your own wall, that is the idea.
