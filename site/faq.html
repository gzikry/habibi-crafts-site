FAQ

Sizes, materials, and how made to order works.

Can I buy something today?

We're not taking orders yet.

What does it cost?

Mugs $14.99. Tees $24.99. Totes $31.99. Onesies $27.99. Prints $23.99. Stickers $5.99. Dad hats $29.99. Free US shipping on orders $39 and up. $6.99 flat below that.

How long until it arrives?

We make it after you order. It usually ships in 2 to 5 days.

What sizes do the tees come in?

Unisex, S through XL. They run true to size. If you are between sizes and want a looser fit, size up.

What sizes do the onesies come in?

White, in three sizes: 3–6 months, 6–12 months, and 12–18 months.

Do the prints come framed?

No. They are 12 × 16 inch matte prints with a white border, so a standard frame fits. The frame is not included.

Do you sell stickers and hats?

Yes. Vinyl stickers are $5.99: Craft Club, Make Something, and Leaf Season. Dad hats are $29.99, and the ones in the shop are black: Habibi Crafts, Make Something, and Leaf Season.

What about sweatshirts, hoodies, and the rest?

They're not in the shop yet.

Are the mugs dishwasher safe?

Yes. The print is applied under a glossy finish, so the mug can go in the dishwasher and the microwave. Hand washing keeps the print crisp for longer.

How do I wash a tee or tote?

Machine wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low. Skip the iron directly on the print. If you need to press it, do it from the inside.

Can I order more than one piece at a time?

Yes. Multiple pieces in a single order is fine, and they ship together.

Something arrived damaged. What now?

Send us a photo within 30 days and we will replace or refund it. Printing or embroidery errors are on us, not on you.

Can I change or cancel an order?

If the order hasn't been made yet, yes. Because pieces are made quickly, message us as soon as you can and we will do our best.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not yet. For now we ship within the United States only.

Will every order include a thank-you card?

Not every box. Apparel, totes, and hats may include a small thank-you card. Mugs, stickers, and prints get a packing-slip note only.

How do I track an order?

When we start taking orders, a tracking note will arrive by email after the carrier scans your package.
