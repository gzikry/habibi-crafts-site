Shipping

Every piece is printed after you order it. Nothing ships from a shelf.

Made to order

We don't keep stock. Your mug, shirt, or tote is printed and packed once you order it, which is why nothing arrives next-day and why we can carry so many designs without waste.

Timing

Printing takes two to five business days for mugs, totes, onesies, and prints, and three to seven for tees. Shipping is added on top of that — usually two to five days within the United States.

You'll get tracking by email as soon as the carrier scans your parcel.

Where we ship

Within the United States. We don't ship internationally yet.

Shipping cost

Calculated at checkout based on what's in the order and where it's going. Multiple pieces in one order ship together.

If something arrives wrong

Send us a photo within 30 days of delivery and we'll reprint or refund it. Printing errors, damaged goods, and misprints are on us.

Change of heart is different. If the order hasn't gone to print yet, we can cancel it — message us quickly, since we start printing fast. Once a personalised item has been printed, it can't be resold, so we can't take it back for preference.
