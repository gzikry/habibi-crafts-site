About FAQ

Privacy

Short version: we collect as little as possible, and we don't sell anything about you.

What we collect

We use Plausible to count page views. It is privacy-friendly by design: no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal profiles, and no data sold or shared with advertisers. It tells us which pages get read, not who read them.

Orders

When you place an order we need your name, shipping address, email, and payment details. Payment is handled by our payment processor — we never see or store your card number. Your address is shared with our printing partner so your order can be produced and shipped, and with the carrier so it can be delivered.

Email

If you write to us, we have your message and your address. We use it to answer you. We don't add you to a mailing list for it.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal information. We don't run advertising networks or retargeting pixels on this site. We don't build profiles of visitors.

Your choices

Ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Reach us through the contact page and we will act on it. If you are in California or the EU, you have specific statutory rights to access, correct, and delete your data, and we honour those requests.

Cookies

This site sets none for analytics. If we add a cart or checkout later, that will need a session cookie to work, and this page will say so before it goes live.

Changes

If how we handle data changes, this page changes with it.
