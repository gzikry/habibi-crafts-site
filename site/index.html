Why this exists

Some phrases deserve a place on the shelf.

Habibi Crafts Co started with the words that show up in everyday life: yalla when it's time to go, sahtein before a meal, and khalas when the conversation is over. We're putting those little pieces of home on useful things, one design at a time.
